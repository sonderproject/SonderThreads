# Instructions for Claude

## Positioning (read first)
- Surfdesk is an everyday organizer for professionals, entrepreneurs and busy people. It keeps your whole day in one place: tasks and to-dos, notes, lists, reminders, calendar, voice capture and the smart command box, plus clients/customers, visits, follow-ups, bookings and reports. Client management is a big part, never the whole story.
- It's for everyone with a lot to keep track of: individuals and busy people, freelancers and entrepreneurs, every kind of business with customers (salons, gyms, coaches, trainers, contractors and trades, consultants, agencies, clinics), and nonprofits. Two organization versions: business ("Visits", "Insights", revenue) and nonprofit ("Check-ins", "Impact", demographics).
- In any copy: lead with everyday organization for professionals and entrepreneurs. The owner describes things casually for context; never quote their wording in copy, write polished marketing copy that means the same thing. Never describe Surfdesk as a case-management, nonprofit-only or client-tracker-only tool; nonprofits are one audience, never the headline.

## Data access
- Tasks (and task history in `activity`) are private to the member who owns them, organization tasks included: scope task queries to `user_id`, never `mineOrOrg`. Organization reports may only count tasks (server-side), never read them.
- Every query gets its `user_id` from `await requireUserId()` (`lib/current-user.ts`). Never import `OWNER_ID` anywhere else — accounts (see `docs/accounts-plan.md`) depend on that one function being the only source of the user id.
- The one exception is the owner command center (`/owner`, `app/(app)/owner/page.tsx`), which reads across all accounts on purpose. It must stay behind `requireOwner()` (`lib/owner.ts`), which 404s for everyone else. Owner = BOTH the email in `OWNER_EMAILS` (Vercel, exactly `sonderproject.co@gmail.com`) AND the account's permanent id (`OWNER_USER_IDS`, default pinned in `lib/owner.ts`); the command center also requires two-step sign-in on that account. Every command-center page and owner action must call `await requireOwner()` itself (a test enforces it) and log what it did with `logOwnerAction()` (which also emails the owner a copy). Suspension and maintenance mode are enforced in `getAuthState()` (`lib/session.ts`), after excluding the owner (`lib/owner-ids.ts`). Owner controls must never be able to suspend or lock out the owner's own account. That's the owner's personal master account: free for life, never in an organization, and the only one with the command center (linked from Settings). The owner's organization uses a separate account.

## Exits
- A client/customer can be exited (`lib/exits.ts`, `lib/actions/exits.ts`, `components/people/PersonExit.tsx`): the person and history stay, `people.exited_on` marks the current exit, every exit is kept in `person_exits` (re-enroll closes it; they can exit again). Reasons are per organization (admins) or per solo account, in Settings → Outcomes & exits, each in a group: positive, neutral or **unplanned** (owner's choice of word, Nov 23, 2026; never "negative"). Each exit stores its group at the time, so regrouping a reason never rewrites past reports. Wording follows the account (`exitAction`, `exitedLabel`, `reopenAction` in `lib/terms.ts`): nonprofits "Exit from program"/"Re-enroll", businesses "Mark as former customer". Exited people are hidden from the people list by default, recent people, "needs attention" and all automations. Impact/Insights and the PDF show exits by reason and the positive/neutral/unplanned split. DB test: `scripts/db-tests/exits.test.ts`.

## Smart forms
- Business only (see Plans). Forms at `/forms` (`lib/forms.ts`, `lib/forms-shared.ts`, `lib/forms-intake.ts`, `lib/actions/forms.ts`, `components/forms/*`): builder with 15 field types, templates per audience (`lib/form-templates.ts`, never SSN/medical/ID questions; a test enforces it), public page `/f/<code>` (link + QR only; no embedding, the frame-ancestors block stays). An organization shares its forms: Admins and Managers build them and see every response; Members see only responses assigned to them. Answers never go in URLs, analytics, logs or notification text.
- Intake: "review" (staff approve), "auto" (new profile only when no email/phone/name match), or "none". Nothing ever overwrites an existing profile; linking adds a note. New profiles always land with an active member of the form's organization (`profileOwner`).
- Spam: honeypot, signed start time (3-second minimum, signed with `CRON_SECRET`), 10 per network per hour, 500 per form per day; caps in `FORM_LIMITS`. Retention purge runs in `/api/cron/reminders`. DB test: `scripts/db-tests/forms.test.ts`.

## Automations (custom rules)
- Business only (see Plans). `/automations` (`lib/workflows-shared.ts` rules and validation, `lib/workflows-run.ts` runner, `lib/workflows.ts` reads and "Test this rule", `lib/actions/workflows.ts`, `components/automations/WorkflowBuilder.tsx`): When → If → Then, rule-based, no AI. The simple follow-ups in Settings → Automations (`lib/automations.ts`) stay separate.
- Triggers: form response, person added (not imports/restores/sample data), person exited, visit logged (queued by database triggers into `automation_events`, only when an enabled rule listens), and time triggers (no contact for N days, birthday, custom date field) checked by `/api/cron/reminders`. No "task overdue" trigger: tasks are private to their owner.
- Actions stay inside the app (task, in-app alert with a fixed message and no client details, note, custom field, follow-up flag, list, assign person/response, response status). There is deliberately no action type for email/SMS to outsiders, deleting, roles/permissions/billing, scripts or webhooks. Every action is re-checked at run time against the rule's own account (members, lists, fields), so a tampered rule can't reach another organization.
- Each rule runs once per event (`workflow_runs` unique `(workflow_id, ref)`), actions never start other rules, exited people are skipped, caps in `WORKFLOW_LIMITS` (100 runs per rule a day, 300 actions per account a day). Org runs are written to `audit_log` (action `automation`). Admins/Managers build them; whoever saves a rule owns it. Don't use `?new=1` on app pages (it opens the quick-add box); use `?start=1`. DB test: `scripts/db-tests/workflows.test.ts`.

## Reports (dashboard + scheduled emails)
- `/reports` (`lib/dashboard-shared.ts` tiles, ranges and schedule rules, `lib/dashboard.ts` tile numbers with member/service/program filters, `lib/reports.ts` access and reads, `lib/reports-run.ts` sender, `lib/actions/reports.ts`, `components/reports/*`). Business only (see Plans): an organization's Admins and Managers; Members and individuals get none (individuals keep their Insights page). Each person picks their own tiles (`dashboard_layouts`). The Impact page stays as the full report and PDF.
- Scheduled reports (`report_schedules`, `report_deliveries` unique per schedule and period): weekly (last 7 days) or monthly (previous month) at a local hour, sent by `/api/cron/reminders`. Recipients are only the schedule's owner or the organization's active Admins and Managers; there is no field for an outside address. Emails hold counts only, never names, notes or free text; an attached PDF drops top visitors and visit reasons. A failed send retries once, then the owner gets an in-app alert. A schedule pauses itself if its owner loses report access. Caps in `REPORT_LIMITS` (10 schedules, 50 emails per account a day). Schedule forms use `components/reports/ActionForm.tsx` (the action returns the next URL and the page reloads): a server-action redirect was occasionally not followed on this page. DB test: `scripts/db-tests/reports.test.ts`.

## Team messages
- Organization members message each other at `/messages` (`lib/messages.ts`, `lib/actions/messages.ts`, `components/messages/*`): channels (every active member reads; Admins/Managers create, rename, archive, set "everyone" or "Admins and Managers only" posting, and can delete messages in them; #general always exists), private group chats and direct chats. Admins can never read a chat they're not in, only delete it (`/messages/manage`, names and counts only); say so wherever chats are described. Clients are tagged as `<#person-id>`, teammates as `<@user-id>`; only the organization's own clients can be tagged, names are filled in per reader ("a client" if they can't see it), and each client tag is written to `audit_log` (action `mention`). Sharing a task posts a copy (tasks stay private); sharing a note posts it. Phone alerts never include message text. Updates poll while a chat is open and visible: every 4 seconds while active, every 15 after a quiet minute, never overlapping (no realtime library). Load-tested Nov 21, 2026 (300 members, 60,000 messages): unread count 0.5 ms, open chat 0.1 ms, refresh 0.03 ms.

## Sign-in security check
- Google/Apple sign-in (`app/auth/callback/route.ts`) skips email confirmation (provider-verified) and records the Terms agreement from the notice under the buttons; email/password sign-ups keep confirmation on. The phone/SMS fields on /signup show only once texts are live (`smsConfigured()`).
- Sign-up, sign-in and password reset use Cloudflare Turnstile through Supabase Auth's CAPTCHA (`components/auth/Turnstile.tsx`). The site key is `NEXT_PUBLIC_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` in Vercel (built into the app: redeploy after changing it); the matching secret key is in Supabase Auth → Attack Protection. Every domain the app runs on must be in the widget's Hostname Management in Cloudflare, or nobody can sign in (error 110200, logged as `auth.captcha_error`, and the owner is emailed). Add any new domain there before pointing it at the app. Backup approved by the owner (Nov 15, 2026): when Supabase refuses the check (`captcha_failed`), sign-in goes through the server with the service key, which Supabase exempts from CAPTCHA (`signInWithoutCheck` in `app/login/actions.ts`: password still checked, 5 tries per email per 15 min, 20 per network per hour, owner emailed). Sign-up, resend confirmation and password reset have the same backup (`allowWithoutCheck`: 3 sign-ups per network an hour, 2 per email an hour).
