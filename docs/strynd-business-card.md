## The product
**Strynd** is one calm app for the people you serve and the work around them: clients, check-ins, services, tasks, notes, calendar and reports. You can type or say something once, and Strynd files it, reminds you and keeps it together.

- **For organizations** (nonprofits, agencies, workforce and training programs): shared client records, check-ins with services, a walk-in kiosk, partner referrals with booked appointments, a Team view, and funder-ready Impact reports (PDF/Excel).
- **For businesses and individuals** (coaches, consultants, trainers, small teams): client history, visits and services with prices, follow-ups, calendar sync (Google/Apple/Outlook), and Insights.
- **Plans:** Individual $9/mo (7-day free trial, no card), Small Team $19/user/mo, Organization $349/mo (20 seats), Enterprise custom.
- **By Sonder Project.** Strynd is a Sonder Project product.

## Brand
- **Logo:** the Sonder Project "S" mark (two interlocking wave shapes). Strynd uses the same mark. It's a single-color shape; use it in one solid color, never with gradients, outlines or shadows.
  - Files in `docs/brand/`:
    - `strynd-mark-blue.png`: blue, transparent background (light cards)
    - `strynd-mark-white.png`: white, transparent background (dark cards)
    - `strynd-mark-black.png`: black, transparent background (one-color print)
    - `strynd-mark-on-dark.png`: preview on the dark background
- **Colors:**
  | Use | Light card | Dark card |
  |---|---|---|
  | Background | Sand `#F6F4EF` | Obsidian `#0A0A09` |
  | Main text | `#141412` | `#F3F0EB` |
  | Secondary text | `#6E5F4D` | `#AEA08D` |
  | Accent (logo, links) | Surf blue `#1C77B5` | `#62B4E6` |
  | Brand surf blue (reference) | `#3E9BD4` | |
- **Fonts:** Outfit (headings and wordmark, Light/Regular, slightly tight letter-spacing) and Inter (body and contact details). Small labels in Inter, uppercase, wide letter-spacing (about 0.22em).
- **Feel:** calm, warm and minimal: lots of space, one blue accent, hairline rules, no heavy effects.

## Print specs
- US standard: **3.5 × 2 in**, plus **0.125 in bleed** on every side (3.75 × 2.25 in file), keeping text **0.125 in** inside the trim.
- **300 DPI**, CMYK for print, export as PDF.
- The logo PNGs are about 1,670 px wide, which is fine up to about 1.5 in wide at 300 DPI. For anything larger, ask for a vector (SVG) version of the mark.
