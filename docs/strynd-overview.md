# Strynd: what it does and why it helps

*Updated November 12, 2026 · www.strynd.com*

**Strynd keeps track of the people you work with and everything you owe them.** You type or say what happened ("Marcus got hired at Amazon", "remind me to call Ana Friday at 3") and Strynd files it in the right place: on the person, on your task list, on your calendar. You open it and see what needs you today.

It's built for two kinds of users:

- **Individuals** who keep track of a lot of people: coaches, mentors, recruiters, program leads, small-business owners and community organizers.
- **Agencies and teams**, such as nonprofits, case-management programs and outreach teams. Staff work their own caseloads, supervisors see the whole picture, and the organization gets reports it can hand to funders.

---

## 1. Fast capture: get it out of your head in seconds

| What you do | What Strynd does |
|---|---|
| Type `Remind me to call Marcus Friday at 3` | Creates a task due Friday at 3 PM, linked to Marcus |
| Type `Marcus got hired at Amazon` | Adds a note to Marcus's profile |
| Type `Call Marcus every Monday` | Creates a repeating weekly task |
| Type `Add Marcus, James and Wes to Group 7` | Creates the group and any new people |
| Paste a paragraph of notes | Splits it into separate notes and tasks |

- **Plain English, no forms.** One command bar handles people, notes, tasks, lists and search. Press `/` anywhere, or tap **+** on a phone.
- **Preview before saving.** You see what it's about to do before you press Enter.
- **Understands dates** like "Friday at 3", "in 2 weeks" and "tomorrow morning", in your own time zone.
- **Voice everywhere.** Tap the mic on any field and talk.
- **Undo on everything.** Complete, delete, snooze or move anything and you get a few seconds to take it back.

**Benefit:** writing things down takes seconds instead of minutes, so things stop slipping through the cracks.

## 2. People and clients

- A profile for each person: name, birthday, phone, email, status, next action and an automatic summary.
- A **timeline** of every note, task, check-in and list they're on.
- Optional demographics for funder reporting: gender, ethnicity and income level.
- A **stale** badge when someone hasn't been touched in 14 days, and a **Needs attention** list for people who are flagged, quiet or have overdue tasks.
- **Import a client list** from Excel or CSV, with automatic column matching, duplicate checks and a preview. **Export** your roster any time.

**Benefit:** you walk into every conversation knowing the history, and nobody goes forgotten.

## 3. Tasks and reminders

- Today, Upcoming, No date and Completed views.
- Due dates with optional times. Tasks can repeat daily, weekly or monthly.
- On a phone, swipe to complete or snooze.
- **Suggested tasks:** Strynd reads your notes for to-dos ("I'll call the landlord tomorrow") and offers to add them with one tap.
- A notification bell for overdue and due-today items, upcoming birthdays and people who need attention.
- *Coming soon:* push notifications and text-message reminders (both built, being switched on).

**Benefit:** follow-ups happen on time without you holding them in your head.

## 4. Notes and lists

- Quick notes, standalone or attached to a person, with search and inline editing.
- Lists for anything (groceries, projects, packing) and **groups** for rosters of people.
- Check items off, drag to reorder, share by text or email, and export to PDF or Excel.
- Turn a list of names into people in one tap. "Marcus Johnson 05/12 5551234567" becomes a profile with his birthday and phone number.

## 5. Calendar, with Google, Apple and Outlook sync

- Month, Week and Agenda views, with Today and jump-to-month buttons.
- Shows your **tasks** (overdue in red) and **birthdays**. You can check off tasks right on the calendar.
- **Show your other calendars in Strynd:** link Google, Apple or Outlook calendars, which also covers Calendly and Cal.com bookings. Their events appear read-only, each in its own color.
- **See Strynd in your other calendars:** subscribe from Google, Apple or Outlook to a private link, and your open tasks show up on your phone's calendar.

**Benefit:** one view of your whole day, without retyping anything.

## 6. Meetings and check-ins

- Log a meeting (called a "check-in" for organizations) with a person, a time and a reason, in seconds.
- Browse them by day, week or month, and export them.
- Every check-in lands on the person's timeline.

## 6b. Services: know what works

- List what you offer in **Settings → Services** (haircut, consultation, catering order, housing support), with an optional price.
- Log one in a tap when you record a visit or meeting, or just type "Ana – haircut" or "Marcus bought catering $200".
- Each profile shows the services that person has had, how often and when.
- **Insights** show each service's use, people, revenue, trend vs the last period, and its busiest day and time. Filter People by service.

**Benefit:** real numbers on what sells, what's growing and when you're busiest, without extra data entry.

## 7. For agencies and teams (Organization plan)

- **Roles:**
  - **Case managers** work their own caseload.
  - **Supervisors** see every client in the organization, read-only.
  - **Admins** also invite staff, change roles and deactivate people.
- **Personal stays personal.** Each staff member's own tasks, notes and lists are never visible to the organization.
- **Team dashboard:** each staff member's clients, open tasks and items needing follow-up.
- **Shared client records** with staff comments. Client records stay with the organization when someone leaves.
- **Activity log:** who did what, and when.
- **Front-desk kiosk:** turn a tablet into a check-in station. Clients type their name and pick a reason for the visit, and staff see it right away. A PIN locks it.
- **Referral link:** a public form partners use to refer clients, with appointment booking, duplicate detection ("might already be a client") and an accept/decline queue.
- **Impact reports** (called "Insights" for individuals):
  - People served (each counted once) and how many were new.
  - Busiest days and hours, reasons for visits and follow-through.
  - Who you serve by gender, ethnicity and income level.
  - Exports to PDF and Excel for funders and boards.

**Benefit:** supervisors get visibility, staff keep their own workspace, and grant reports take minutes instead of a weekend.

## 8. Works where you work

- Install it on any phone or computer from the browser ("Add to Home Screen"). It gets its own icon and runs full-screen.
- On Android, share text from other apps straight into Strynd.
- Keyboard shortcuts: `/` for commands, `Ctrl/⌘+K` to search everything.
- Light and dark mode.

## 9. Security, privacy and your data

- Encrypted in transit and at rest. Database-level rules keep each account's data separate.
- Two-step sign-in, automatic sign-out after inactivity, and "sign out everywhere".
- **Your data is yours:** export everything any time (CSV, Excel, or a full JSON backup). Delete your account and its data in one step.
- We never sell your data or use it for ads.
- Clear Terms and Privacy policy, agreed to at sign-up and recorded.

## 10. Help when you need it

- **Contact support** from inside the app (Settings → About & help). Messages go straight to the founder, with your plan and device attached so you don't have to explain.
