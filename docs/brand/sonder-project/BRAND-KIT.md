## 1. Who we are

**Sonder Project is a software studio and holding company.** It designs, builds and owns its own products. The company's job is to find everyday problems busy people have, build a calm, useful product for each one, and run it for the long term.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Software studio / holding company (parent of independent product brands) |
| **Products** | **Surfdesk** (live, thesurfdesk.app): everyday organizer for professionals, entrepreneurs and teams. **Sonder Protocol** (planned) |
| **Founder** | DeAnte Spruill, Founder |
| **Contact** | dante@sonderproject.co (also Surfdesk support) |
| **Location** | California, USA |
| **Industry (LinkedIn etc.)** | Software Development |
| **Legal status** | Sole proprietorship until the LLC forms (planned January 2027) |

### The name
*Sonder* is the realization that every passerby lives a life as vivid and complex as your own. It's the studio's point of view: every user is a real person with a full, busy life, and good software respects that by saving their time and protecting their data.

- Write it **Sonder Project**: two words, both capitalized. Never "SonderProject", "Sonder Projects", "The Sonder Project", or "Sonder" alone in formal copy.
- Say it "SON-der PROJ-ect" (*sonder* rhymes with *wander*).

### Mission *(proposed)*
**Build calm, useful software that helps busy people run their work and their lives.**

### Vision *(proposed)*
A small family of products that people rely on every day, each one simple, private and worth paying for.

### Values
1. **Respect people's time.** Every feature should save more time than it costs.
2. **Calm over clutter.** Fewer, better things; quiet design; no dark patterns (design tricks that push people into choices they didn't mean to make).
3. **Privacy by default.** Data is never sold or used for ads; people can export or delete everything.
4. **Built to last.** Careful engineering, honest pricing, products we own and run ourselves.

### How we build
- **Own the whole product:** design, engineering, billing, support and brand, in-house.
- **Start with one real user group,** then widen (Surfdesk started with a workforce program and grew into an organizer for every business and individual).
- **Ship, measure, refine.** Small releases, real usage data, no vanity features.

## 2. Brand architecture

Sonder Project uses an **endorsed brand architecture**: each product has its own name, look and audience, and the parent quietly vouches for it (like "Instagram from Meta").

| Where | What leads | How Sonder Project appears |
|---|---|---|
| Product website, app, ads, social | The product (Surfdesk) | Small: "by Sonder Project", footer "A Sonder Project company" |
| LinkedIn company page, partnerships, hiring, investors | Sonder Project | Full logo; products listed as "our products" |
| Legal (terms, privacy, contracts, Stripe, app stores) | Sonder Project (the legal entity) | Full legal name |
| Business cards | Product card: product leads, "A Sonder Project company" on the back. Studio card: Sonder Project leads | |

**Visual relationship:** all products share the S-wave mark with the parent for now. The difference is the wordmark and the default ground: **Sonder Project leans dark (obsidian)** with an uppercase spaced wordmark; **Surfdesk leans light (sand)** with a title-case wordmark. A future product may get its own mark; if it shares this one, it must use its own wordmark.

**Rules**
- Never put the Sonder Project logo larger than, or before, a product logo on product material.
- Never merge names ("Sonder Surfdesk", "Surfdesk Project").
- New product names are chosen on their own merits and don't need "Sonder" in them.

## 3. Visual identity

### 3.1 Logo
**The mark:** an "S" built from two interlocking waves: motion and flow, two parts working as one.

**Primary logo:** mark + **SONDER PROJECT** in Outfit Light (300), uppercase, letter-spacing 0.25em, vertically centered on the mark. The gap between mark and wordmark is about a third of the mark's height.

| File (in `docs/brand/sonder-project/logos/`) | Use |
|---|---|
| `sonder-project-lockup-on-dark` | **Primary.** Light-surf mark, bone wordmark, for obsidian and dark photos |
| `sonder-project-lockup-blue` | Deep-surf mark, ink wordmark, for sand, white and light photos |
| `sonder-project-lockup-white` / `-black` | One-color versions (embossing, single-color print, partner logo walls) |
| `sonder-project-avatar` (400²) | Profile picture: bone mark on obsidian (LinkedIn company logo, X, GitHub organization) |
| `sonder-project-linkedin-cover` (1512×256) | LinkedIn company cover: "We build calm software / for busy people." + MAKERS OF SURFDESK |
| `mark-{surf-blue,blue,light-blue,black,white,sand}` | The mark alone (favicon, watermark, small spaces) |

All SVGs are fully outlined (no fonts needed). PNGs are transparent. Rebuild: `python3 docs/brand/tools/make_logos.py` then `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node docs/brand/tools/render_pngs.js`.

- **Clear space:** half the mark's height on every side.
- **Minimum size:** horizontal logo 140px wide on screen (1.25 in print). Smaller than that, use the mark alone (16px / 0.25 in minimum).
- **Do:** one solid color; bone or light surf on obsidian; ink or deep surf on sand.
- **Don't:** gradients, outlines, shadows, glows, 3D; rotating, stretching or flipping; retyping the wordmark or changing its spacing; deep surf on obsidian (too little contrast); busy backgrounds without a solid panel.

### 3.2 Color
Dark-first. The palette is shared with Surfdesk; the default use is flipped.

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Obsidian** | `#0A0A09` | Primary background |
| **Charcoal** | `#141412` | Cards and panels on obsidian |
| **Stone line** | `#2A2926` | Hairline borders on dark |
| **Bone** | `#F3F0EB` | Main text and the mark on dark |
| **Stone** | `#AEA08D` | Secondary text and labels on dark |
| **Light surf** | `#62B4E6` | The single accent on dark: links, highlights, key word in a headline |
| **Surf blue** | `#3E9BD4` | Brand reference blue (illustration, print, photos) |
| **Sand** | `#F6F4EF` | Light-version background (documents, letterhead) |
| **Ink** | `#141412` | Text on sand |
| **Umber** | `#6E5F4D` | Secondary text on sand |
| **Deep surf** | `#1C77B5` | Accent on sand |

- About **80% obsidian, 17% bone/stone text, 3% blue.** Blue marks one thing per layout.
- Documents meant for printing (letters, invoices, one-pagers) use the sand version.

### 3.3 Typography
| Role | Font | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark, labels | **Outfit** Light 300 / **Inter** 500 | UPPERCASE, wordmark +0.25em, labels +0.22em |
| Headlines | Outfit Light 300 | Sentence case, -0.02em, line-height 1.05 |
| Body | Inter 400 | 16px, line-height 1.55 |
| Numbers | Inter | Tabular figures |

Both fonts are free on Google Fonts. Fallbacks: system-ui, Helvetica, Arial.

**Signature layout:** a small uppercase spaced label (eyebrow) → a large Outfit Light statement in two lines, the second line in light surf → generous empty space → a hairline rule. Example:

> MAKERS OF SURFDESK
> We build calm software
> **for busy people.**

### 3.4 Imagery and motion
- Product screenshots (on charcoal cards) and real working moments in natural light. Founder photos: natural, unposed, warm light, plain backgrounds.
- No stock "tech" imagery: no circuit boards, glowing brains, robots, neon gradients.
- The large faint mark as a watermark (about 9% opacity), cropped off one edge, is the studio's signature background device (see the LinkedIn cover).
- Motion: slow fades and short rises (0.3–0.5s, ease-out). Nothing bounces.

## 4. Voice

**Founder-led, calm, precise, confident, understated.** Sonder Project sounds like a thoughtful builder explaining what they make and why.

| We are | We aren't |
|---|---|
| Clear and specific | Buzzwordy ("synergy", "disrupt", "leverage") |
| Confident, quiet | Hype ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "10x") |
| Human, first person ("we", "I" for the founder) | Corporate passive voice |
| Honest about stage and size | Inflated ("global leader", "industry-leading") |

**Rules**
1. Lead with the problem people have, then the product that fixes it.
2. Use "we" for the company. In founder posts, "I" is fine and preferred.
3. Prove with products and outcomes, not adjectives.
4. Short sentences, no exclamation marks, no emoji in formal material.
5. When describing Surfdesk, use the Surfdesk kit's positioning: an everyday organizer for professionals, entrepreneurs and busy people (tasks, notes, calendar, clients, team, referrals, reports). Never describe it as case management or a nonprofit-only tool.

### Approved copy
**One line:** Sonder Project is a software studio building calm, useful tools for busy people.

**Short about (LinkedIn "About", ~60 words):**
Sonder Project designs and builds software that helps professionals, entrepreneurs and teams stay organized. We own and run our products end to end, with a focus on simplicity, privacy and craft. Our first product, Surfdesk, keeps your whole workday in one place: tasks, notes, calendar, clients, team and reports. Try it at thesurfdesk.app.

**Long about (~120 words):**
Sonder Project is a software studio founded by DeAnte Spruill. We build products for people with full, busy lives: the owner running a shop, the consultant juggling clients, the team keeping track of a hundred moving parts. Each product does one job well, respects people's time, and treats their data with care: never sold, always theirs to export or delete.
Our first product is Surfdesk, an everyday organizer for professionals and entrepreneurs. It brings tasks, notes, reminders, calendar, clients, bookings, referrals, team management and one-click reports into one calm app. More products are on the way.

**Taglines:**
- We build calm software for busy people. *(proposed, primary)*

- Software that respects your time.
