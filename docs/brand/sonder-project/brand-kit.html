Long about

Sonder Project is a software studio founded by DeAnte Spruill. We build products for people with full, busy lives: the owner running a shop, the consultant juggling clients, the team keeping track of a hundred moving parts. Each product does one job well, respects people's time, and treats their data with care: never sold, always theirs to export or delete. Our first product is Surfdesk, an everyday organizer for professionals and entrepreneurs. WHO WE ARE
Sonder Project is a software studio and holding company. It designs, builds and owns its own products, end to end. Its first product is Surfdesk (thesurfdesk.app), an everyday organizer for professionals, entrepreneurs and busy people. Future ventures (for example the planned Sonder Protocol) will sit under Sonder Project, each with its own name and brand.

MISSION (proposed)
Build calm, useful software that helps busy people run their work and their lives.

THE NAME
"Sonder" is the realization that every passerby lives a life as vivid and complex as your own. We build for real people with full, busy lives, and we respect their time and their data.

VALUES
Respect people's time. Calm over clutter. Privacy by default. Built to last.

HOW WE RELATE TO OUR PRODUCTS
Products lead; Sonder Project signs. On product material we appear small ("Surfdesk by Sonder Project", "A Sonder Project company"). On Sonder Project material (LinkedIn company page, partners, hiring, investors, legal), the products appear as "our products" / "our first product, Surfdesk".

LOOK
Dark-first, calm, minimal. Obsidian #0A0A09 background, bone #F3F0EB text, stone #AEA08D secondary text, light surf blue #62B4E6 as the single accent (surf blue #3E9BD4 for reference, deep surf #1C77B5 on light backgrounds). Light version: sand #F6F4EF with ink #141412 text. Headings in Outfit Light (300); body in Inter. The wordmark and small labels are uppercase with wide letter-spacing (wordmark +0.25em, labels +0.22em). Lots of space, hairline rules, no gradients, no heavy shadows, no emoji.

LOGO
The S-wave mark (two interlocking waves forming an S) next to "SONDER PROJECT" in Outfit Light, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.25em. Always one solid color: bone or light surf on obsidian; ink or deep surf on sand. Never gradients, outlines, shadows, rotation or stretching.

VOICE
Founder-led, calm, precise, confident and understated. Short sentences. Show the work (products, results) instead of big claims. No hype words ("disrupt", "revolutionary", "game-changing"), no slang, no exclamation marks.

KEY LINES
- "We build calm software for busy people." (proposed)
- "Makers of Surfdesk."

FACTS
Founder: DeAnte Spruill. Contact: dante@sonderproject.co. Based in California. Currently a sole proprietorship; an LLC is planned for January 2027.
