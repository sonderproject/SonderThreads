What Surfdesk is

An everyday organizer for professionals and entrepreneurs.

Tasks, notes, lists, reminders, calendar and a command box you type or speak into, plus clients, visits, online booking, referrals, team management and one-click reports. Live at thesurfdesk.app.

Name: Surfdesk, one word, capital S only (never SurfDesk or TheSurfdesk). Parent: a Sonder Project product, credited small: "Surfdesk by Sonder Project" or "A Sonder Project company". Surfdesk always leads.

Positioning

One calm app instead of five.

Lead with everyday organization; then show the depth. Client management is a big part, never the whole story.

Everything in one place

Tasks, notes, lists, calendar, clients and your team, instead of a stack of separate apps.

Nothing slips

Reminders, follow-ups and tasks suggested from your notes keep every commitment in view.

See and prove results

Logo

The S-wave mark.

Two interlocking waves forming an S: motion and flow, two parts working as one. Always one solid color. Clear space equals half the mark's height on every side.

Color

Warm neutrals, one blue.

Sand by day, obsidian by night, and a single surf-blue accent for the one thing that should be noticed. Status colors mark state only, never decoration. Click a value to copy it.

Proportion

About 70% background · 25% text · 5% blue. Full light and dark UI tokens (warn, urgent, new, danger, charts, calendar) are in BRAND-KIT.md section 4.2.

Typography

Outfit for presence, Inter for work.

Both free on Google Fonts. Outfit Light carries the brand and headlines with tight spacing; Inter handles everything people read and tap. Sentence case everywhere except small labels.
