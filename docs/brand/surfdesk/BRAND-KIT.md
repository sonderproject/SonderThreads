## 2. Positioning

### What it is (master description)
**Surfdesk is an everyday organizer for professionals, entrepreneurs and busy people.** It keeps your whole workday in one place: tasks and to-dos, notes, lists, reminders and calendar, with a smart command box you can type or speak into. It also manages the people you work with: clients and customers, visits, follow-ups, online booking, referrals, your team, and reports that show your results in one click.

### The core idea
**Say it once. Surfdesk files it, reminds you and keeps it together.**

### Who it's for
- **Individuals and busy people:** anyone juggling work, side projects, family and appointments.
- **Freelancers and entrepreneurs:** consultants, coaches, realtors, creators, solo founders.
- **Every business with customers:** salons and barbershops, gyms and trainers, studios, coaches and tutors, contractors and trades, consultants and agencies, clinics, groomers and pet care, repair shops.
- **Teams:** any group that shares clients and needs to see who did what.
- **Nonprofits and agencies:** programs and community organizations that need Impact reports for funders. One audience among many, never the headline.

### Two organization versions (same app, different words)
| | Business organization | Nonprofit / agency organization | Individual (no org) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visits are called | **Visits** | **Check-ins** | **Meetings** |
| Reports are called | **Insights** | **Impact** | **Insights** |
| Report shows | Customers served, new vs repeat, busiest times, **revenue** from service prices; no demographics | People served, new vs returning, busiest times, **demographics** for funders and boards | Same as business |
| Everything else | Identical: clients, tasks, notes, team roles, kiosk, referrals, booking, exports | | |

### What it never is
- Not case-management software. Not a nonprofit-only tool. Not "just a client tracker".
- Not a CRM in the sales sense (no pipelines, deal stages or sales quotas). Avoid the word "CRM" in copy.
- Not "AI-powered" as a pitch. Describe the outcome ("type or say it once") instead.

### Three outcomes (use everywhere)
1. **Everything in one place.** Tasks, notes, calendar, clients and team, instead of five apps.
2. **Nothing slips.** Reminders, follow-ups and suggested tasks keep every commitment visible.
3. **See and prove your results.** One-click Impact and Insights reports (PDF/Excel) for funders, boards, partners or your own growth.

### Elevator pitches
**10 seconds:** "Surfdesk is one app for your whole workday: tasks, notes, calendar, clients and your team. Type or say it once and it's organized."

**30 seconds:** "Most professionals juggle a to-do app, a notes app, a calendar and a spreadsheet of clients. Surfdesk puts it all in one place. Type or say 'Call Maria Tuesday at 3pm' and it becomes a task with a reminder, linked to Maria. It tracks every client's visits and follow-ups, lets clients book you online, handles referrals, manages your team's roles, and turns your work into a polished report in one click. It's $9 a month, with a free trial."

**60 seconds:** add: "It works for a solo consultant and for a 50-person organization. Businesses get Insights with revenue; nonprofits get Impact reports with demographics for funders. Teams share one client list with Admin, Manager and Member roles, invite everyone in one step, and each person's tasks stay private. Data is never sold, it's encrypted, and you can export or delete everything any time. Works on any phone or computer."

### Competitive frame
Surfdesk replaces a stack: to-do app (Todoist, Apple Reminders) + notes (Notion, Google Keep) + calendar + spreadsheet or CRM for clients + a booking link + a report you build by hand. The pitch is **one calm app instead of five**, not "better than X".

## 3. Product facts

### Feature list (everything, grouped)
**Everyday organization**
- Smart command box: type or speak plain English ("Remind me to call Marcus Friday at 3", "add milk, eggs and coffee to groceries"). Live preview, smart dates, brain dumps (several items at once), `@` people and `#` lists autocomplete, `/` to open, `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` to search.
- Voice capture on any text field (hold **+** on a phone).
- Tasks: Today, Upcoming, No date, Completed; due dates and times; repeats; swipe to complete or snooze; bulk select; Undo everywhere.
- Notes, standalone or on a person; to-dos inside notes become **suggested tasks**.
- Lists and groups: check off, reorder, share, export to PDF/Excel, turn a list into people.
- Calendar: tasks, birthdays, visits; sync with Google, Apple and Outlook calendars.
- Reminders: in-app notifications; push and text reminders (built; keys and Twilio still to turn on).
- Spell check and optional autocorrect.
- Installs to the home screen like an app; works on any phone or computer.

**Clients and customers**
- A profile for every client: contact info, birthday, status, next action, auto-written summary, full timeline of notes, tasks and visits.
- Visits / check-ins / meetings with services and prices; one-tap logging; walk-in kiosk.
- Custom fields on people.
- Follow-ups and "needs attention" (overdue, quiet for a while, flagged).
- Services drill-down: everyone who got a service, with editable dates; save them as a group or list.
- Email everyone on a list.
- CSV import and export.

**Growth**
- Online booking link (`thesurfdesk.app/book/...`): clients pick a time.
- Referrals: partner organizations or people refer clients through a link; confirmation emails; booked appointments.
- Automations: simple rules that create follow-ups automatically.

**Teams**
- Shared workspace and client list. Roles: **Admin**, **Manager**, **Member**.
- Bulk invites (paste or CSV, with roles), a team join link for Members, bulk role changes.
- Team view: each person's clients, today's visits/check-ins, calendar; activity log.
- Managers read client records and comment; nobody sees anyone else's tasks or personal notes.
- One bill for the whole team.

**Reports**
- Impact (nonprofit) or Insights (business) dashboards: people served, new vs returning, services, outcomes, busiest times, revenue (business) or demographics (nonprofit), monthly table, all-time totals, up to a year back and more.
- One-click PDF and Excel reports.

**Trust**
- Data is never sold or used for ads. Encrypted in transit and at rest. Two-step sign-in. Automatic sign-out on shared computers. Database-level access rules separate every account and organization. Full export and account deletion at any time.

## 4. Visual identity

### 4.1 Logo

**The mark:** an "S" built from two interlocking waves: motion and flow, two parts working as one. Surfdesk shares the mark with its parent, Sonder Project; the Surfdesk wordmark next to it is what makes it Surfdesk.

**Lockups**
| File (in `docs/brand/surfdesk/logos/`) | Use |
|---|---|
| `surfdesk-lockup-{blue,on-dark,black,white}` | **Primary** Surfdesk logo: mark + "Surfdesk" (Outfit Light, -0.02em) |
| `surfdesk-stacked-{…}` | Square-ish spaces, end cards, posters: mark over wordmark over "BY SONDER PROJECT" |
| `surfdesk-header-lockup-{…}` | Small, refined uses (app header, footers): mark + "SURFDESK" uppercase, +0.25em |
| `mark-{surf-blue,blue,light-blue,black,white,sand}` | The mark alone |
| `surfdesk-app-icon` (1024²) | App icon / store icon: light-blue mark on obsidian |
| `surfdesk-avatar-sand`, `surfdesk-avatar-dark` (400²) | Social profile pictures (Instagram, LinkedIn page logo, X, Facebook) |
| `surfdesk-linkedin-cover` (1512×256) | LinkedIn page covers (logo overlaps bottom-left, so content sits right) |
| `favicon` | Browser tab icon |

Color suffixes: **blue** = for light backgrounds (mark `#1C77B5`, text `#141412`); **on-dark** = for dark backgrounds (mark `#62B4E6`, text `#F3F0EB`); **black** / **white** = one-color.

Every SVG is fully outlined (no fonts needed) and works in any design tool. PNGs are transparent. To rebuild after a change: `python3 docs/brand/tools/make_logos.py` then `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node docs/brand/tools/render_pngs.js`.

**Clear space:** keep empty space around any logo equal to **half the mark's height** on every side.

**Minimum size:** mark 16 px tall on screen (favicon), 0.25 in in print. Horizontal lockup 100 px wide on screen, 1 in in print. Below that, use the mark alone.

**Do**
- Use one solid color from the palette.
- Put the blue logo on sand, white or light photos; the on-dark logo on obsidian or dark photos.
- Keep the mark and wordmark proportions exactly as supplied.

**Don't**
- Don't add gradients, outlines, drop shadows, glows or 3D.
- Don't rotate, skew, stretch, recolor parts of the mark, or flip it.
- Don't retype the wordmark in another font or change its spacing.
- Don't put the mark on a busy background without a solid panel behind it.
- Don't lock the mark up with another product name without approval.
- Don't use the old Strynd files (`docs/brand/strynd-mark-*.png`) for new work; use `docs/brand/surfdesk/logos/`.

| Role | Font | Weight | Size / spacing |

- Icons: simple line icons (1.5–2px stroke, rounded ends). **No emoji as icons.**

## 5. Voice and copy

### Personality
**Calm, capable, warm, clear.** Surfdesk sounds like a sharp, organized friend who has everything handled, not a hype machine.

| We are | We aren't |
|---|---|
| Clear | Clever for its own sake |
| Confident | Salesy or pushy |
| Warm | Cute or slangy |
| Concise | Vague or padded |
| Specific ("Call Maria Tuesday at 3pm") | Abstract ("leverage productivity synergies") |

### Writing rules
1. Lead with the everyday benefit (organized, nothing slips, one app), then show the depth (clients, team, referrals, reports).
2. Short sentences. One idea per line. Periods, not exclamation marks.
3. Show, don't claim: a concrete example beats an adjective.
4. Buttons say exactly what happens: "Start your free trial", "Log a visit", "Download report".
5. Use "you" and "your". Say "we" only for Sonder Project / the company.
6. Match the audience's word: customers (business), clients (services, nonprofits), people (general).
7. Never quote the founder's casual wording; always rewrite into polished copy that means the same thing.
8. No discount pressure or countdowns. One clear, honest offer (free trial, or 30% off to skip it).

### Words
| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| organized, in one place, nothing slips, follow-up, everyday, workday | to the max, what you've got going on, game-changer, revolutionary |
| clients / customers / people | users (in marketing), leads (unless sales context) |
| visits (business), check-ins (nonprofit), meetings (individual) | the wrong term for the audience |
| Impact report (nonprofit), Insights report (business) | "analytics suite" |
| type or say it once | AI-powered, AI magic |
| team, roles: Admin / Manager / Member | case manager, supervisor (in UI/copy), CRM |
| private, never sold, encrypted | "military-grade", "100% secure" |

### Legal tone
Plain English, accurate, never overpromising (we store data to run the app, so we never say "we don't keep your data").
