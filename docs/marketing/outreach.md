# Outreach starter

Goal: 50 organizations and 50 local businesses in a simple list, contacted personally, 10 a day. Personal emails beat ads at this stage.

## Build the first list (about 2 hours)
**Organizations:** search Google Maps and your county's 211 directory for "workforce development", "job training", "community center", "family resource center", "reentry program" near you. For each, note: name, website, director's name (About/Team page or LinkedIn), email or contact form.
**Businesses:** Google Maps for salons, barbers, groomers, vets, gyms, tutors within 10 miles; favor independents with 4.5★+ and lots of reviews (they have regulars).
Keep it in a Google Sheet: Name · Type · Contact · Email · Date sent · Reply · Next step.

Send from **dante@sonderproject.co** (not no-reply). Max ~20 new emails a day, to protect your email reputation.

## Follow-up (5 days later, reply to the same thread)

## Rules
- Personalize the first line every time (something from their website or reviews).
- Never buy email lists, and stop after one follow-up.
- Every email includes who you are and a real way to reply (CAN-SPAM).
