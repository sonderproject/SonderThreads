# Surfdesk positioning

*Nov 13, 2026. The source for every ad, post, email and pitch.*

## One line
**Surfdesk remembers every person you serve: who they are, what you did for them, and what's next.**

## Who it's for (two lanes)

### Lane 1: Organizations (lead with this)
Nonprofits, workforce and training programs, community agencies, case-management teams, drop-in centers.
- **Pain:** client info lives in spreadsheets, sticky notes and three different apps; reports for funders take days; nobody knows what happened yesterday.
- **Surfdesk gives them:** one shared client record, check-ins with the service delivered, a walk-in kiosk, partner referrals with booked appointments, a Team view of today's work, and Impact reports (PDF/Excel) ready for funders.
- **Buyer:** program director or executive director. **User:** front-desk and program staff.
- **Plan:** Small Team ($19/user/mo) or Organization ($349/mo, 20 seats).

### Lane 2: Businesses that live on regulars
Salons, barbershops, groomers, vets and pet care, trainers and gyms, tutors, studios, coaches, repair shops: any business where the same customers come back.
- **Pain:** the booking app knows *when* someone comes in, not *who they are*. Preferences, last visit, what they bought, their dog's name, their kid's birthday: all in someone's head.
- **Surfdesk gives them:** a profile for every regular with full visit history, services and amounts, notes, birthdays and follow-ups, plus Insights on busiest days, top services and who hasn't been back.
- **Positioning:** *works alongside your booking app.* Keep Square, Vagaro or Booksy for appointments and payments; Surfdesk is the memory. (Surfdesk doesn't take bookings or payments, and doesn't need to.)
- **Buyer:** the owner. **Plan:** Individual ($9/mo) or Small Team.

## Three outcomes (use these everywhere)
1. **Know every person.** Their story, history and what's next, in one place.
2. **Log it in seconds.** Type or say it once; Surfdesk files it. Check-ins are one tap.
3. **Prove it.** Reports that show who you served and what you delivered, for funders or for your own growth.

## Proof points
- Used every day by a real workforce program. (Ask them before naming them publicly; a short quote from their director is the best proof you can have.)
- Private by design: each person's tasks and notes stay theirs; data is never sold; database-level access rules.
- Works on any phone or computer; installs to the home screen.
- 7-day free trial, no card (Individual); 14-day trial for teams; 30% off the first month to skip the trial.

## Words to use / avoid
- **Use:** clients or customers (match the audience), regulars, check-in, visit, services, impact, "in one place", "in seconds".
- **Avoid:** CRM (sounds like sales software), AI-powered (say what it does instead), "case management" for businesses.

## Say the name
**Surfdesk** rhymes with *grinned*. Say it at the start of every video.

## Call to action
**Start free at thesurfdesk.app.** Organizations: "Start a 14-day team trial."
