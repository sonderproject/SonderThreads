# Strynd promo video (Kling AI)

Two cuts from one prompt style: **Organizations** (30s) and **Regulars** (15s). Generate each shot separately in Kling (5–10s clips), then join them in CapCut or Kling's editor and add the text overlays and voiceover there. Kling makes better shots than whole ads.

**Look for every shot:** calm, warm, natural light; sand and soft-blue palette (#F6F4EF, #1C77B5); real-feeling people, no stock-photo smiles; slow camera moves; no text generated inside the video (add text in the editor, Kling garbles letters).

## Real app footage
For the app itself, use the screenshots in `docs/marketing/screenshots/` (or screen-record strynd.com on your phone) instead of AI shots, so the product looks exactly like it is. Pan slowly across them in the editor (Ken Burns effect).

## Settings
- Aspect: **9:16** for Reels/TikTok/Shorts, **16:9** for LinkedIn/YouTube. Generate 9:16 first.
- Music: soft, warm acoustic or lo-fi; low under the voiceover.
- Voiceover: your own voice reads as more trustworthy than AI for a founder-led brand.
